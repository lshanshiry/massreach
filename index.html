Safety and privacy

Independent site. MassReach is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or paid by the programs listed here.

No guarantees. We collect details from public pages, but they can be wrong or out of date. Each program is responsible for its own rules, safety, and decisions.

Check before you apply. Use the official page, and talk with a parent, guardian, counselor, or another trusted adult before you sign up or meet anyone.

Protect yourself. Be careful with any program that asks for money, your Social Security number, or bank details early on, or wants to move the conversation to a private chat. Stop and tell a trusted adult if something feels wrong.

Privacy. MassReach has no accounts. Your search choices stay on the page, and your saved list stays in your browser on this device.
